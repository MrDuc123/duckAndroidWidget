One widget, two screens

The widget checks how much room it has and picks a layout: a single list on the narrow cover screen, two columns when the phone is open. You add it once per screen.

Where the news comes from

Every hour, a free GitHub job collects AI news from RSS feeds and saves it as news.json. Both phones read that one file.

How fresh it is

The iPhone widget redraws every 15 minutes, the Galaxy widget about every 30. Either one updates right away when you tap refresh, and the mascot's mood moves on by itself as the top story gets older.

Color-coded categories

Research Industry Community

Each headline carries its category icon and color, plus a round initial badge for its source (HN, aX, TC…). Tapping a headline opens the full article in your browser.

Change it any time

On a real phone, touch and hold the widget and open its settings: Edit Widget on iPhone, the settings button on Galaxy. Pick the duck or box cat, peeking or faded behind the text, and a colour. Try it here: press and hold (or right-click) either widget.

The real apps

This page is a preview. The working widgets are two projects in your Documents folder: AINewsiOS opens in Xcode, and AINewsWidget opens in Android Studio. Both use the same mascots and sample headlines.
